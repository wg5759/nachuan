# Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique code and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently.
- Respect privacy, licenses, security embargoes, and user consent.
- Make space for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, credential/customer-data disclosure, and publication of an
unpatched exploit are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact project maintainers through a private
repository channel. For vulnerabilities or security-sensitive conduct, use the
private process in SECURITY.md. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed
to protect the community or users.
